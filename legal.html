4. Returns and Refunds

Because all products are handmade and built-to-order, returns are only accepted within 7 days of delivery and only for manufacturing defects. Requests must include:

  • Order ID and purchase date

No returns will be accepted for misuse, modification, water damage, or physical damage caused by the user.

5. Warranty Options

There are two available warranty types:

  • Manufacturer Warranty (30 Days, Free) – Covers manufacturing defects under normal use. Excludes damage, modification, or abuse.
  • Extended Warranty (1 Year, $4.99) – Covers all accidental or normal-use damages except deliberate destruction or extreme neglect.

All warranty claims must include:

  • Order ID and date of purchase
  • Customer name
  • Detailed description of damage
  • Photo or video evidence

Warranty processing requires time for verification and documentation.

The buyer or their legal representative may contact PennCamm with their order ID, purchase date, and supporting photos for supplier follow-up.
